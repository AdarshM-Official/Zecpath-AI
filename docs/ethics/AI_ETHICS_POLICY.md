# AI Ethics Policy

## 1. Consent Requirements
All candidates interacting with the HR AI system must provide explicit, opt-in consent before the interview begins. 
*   **Transparency:** Candidates must be informed that they are speaking with an AI.
*   **Data Usage:** Candidates must be informed how their voice, transcript, and scoring data will be used in the hiring process.
*   **Opt-out:** Candidates must have a clear path to opt out of AI screening and request a human alternative.

## 2. Transparency & Explainability
The AI system does not act as a "black box".
*   Every score generated (ATS relevance, screening, communication, confidence, consistency) is accompanied by an explainability breakdown.
*   Human recruiters are provided with clear notes detailing *why* a score was given (e.g., "Relevance score based on 75% keyword overlap with the job description").

## 3. Human-in-the-Loop Oversight
This AI is designed as a **decision-support tool**, not a final decision-maker.
*   All automated rejections or approvals must be reviewed by a human recruiter.
*   The system generates risk flags and behavioral indicators for human review, rather than automatically disqualifying candidates based on these metrics.

## 4. Bias Mitigation
The system is explicitly programmed to ignore demographic signals.
*   The text analysis focuses strictly on objective metrics: keyword overlap, answer consistency, and structural completeness.
*   PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is scrubbed from transcripts before processing to prevent unintended bias based on names, locations, or demographic indicators.
