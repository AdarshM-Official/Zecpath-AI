## 2. Simulated Stakeholder Q&A

### Q1 (From the CTO): "How do you stop a candidate from just reading answers generated by ChatGPT on a second monitor?"
- **Weak Initial Answer:** "We monitor their browser."
- **Refined Answer:** "Zecpath-AI employs a multi-modal `MalpracticeDetector`. If a candidate uses a second monitor, our Vision Model tracks the repeated >30° gaze deviations. Furthermore, if they try to copy-paste from ChatGPT, the frontend traps the paste event, and the AI immediately correlates high-accuracy answers with low-logical reasoning and fast response times. This combination automatically overrides their score and flags them for rejection."

### Q2 (From the VP of HR): "Does your communication scoring penalize non-native English speakers?"
- **Weak Initial Answer:** "Yes, it checks for grammar and fluency."
- **Refined Answer:** "Fairness is a core pillar. The system allows recruiters to toggle a `language_proficiency` modifier for specific roles. This relaxes the penalty weights on strict grammar and hesitation markers (like 'uh' or 'um'), ensuring we evaluate the candidate strictly on their technical logic and structural clarity, rather than native fluency."

### Q3 (From the CFO): "What is the infrastructure cost to run this at scale?"
- **Refined Answer:** "Because we use lightweight regex/NLP parsing for the early screening and a stateless API architecture, the per-candidate compute cost is fractions of a cent compared to human recruiter hours. The heaviest component is the Docker sandbox, which scales horizontally on demand."
