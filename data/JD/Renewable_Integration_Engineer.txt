87. Renewable Integration Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Renewable Integration Engineer focuses on integrating renewable energy sources like solar 
and wind into existing electrical grids efficiently and safely. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Integrate solar, wind, and hybrid systems into grids  
• Analyze grid stability with renewable penetration  
• Design energy storage integration (battery systems)  
• Manage variability and intermittency of renewables  
• Perform power flow and stability studies  
• Ensure compliance with grid codes and standards  
Required Skills 
• Power systems analysis  
• Renewable energy technologies  
• Energy storage systems  
• Simulation tools (MATLAB, ETAP)  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• M.Tech in Power Systems preferred
