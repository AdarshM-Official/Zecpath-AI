74. Electrical Project Manager 
Job Summary 
An Electrical Project Manager is responsible for end-to-end management of electrical 
projects, including planning, execution, budgeting, and delivery. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Plan and execute large-scale electrical projects  
• Manage budgets, schedules, and resources  
• Lead engineering and site teams  
• Coordinate with clients, consultants, and contractors  
• Ensure project compliance with safety and quality standards  
• Monitor project risks and implement mitigation strategies  
• Approve technical decisions and design changes  
• Deliver projects within scope, time, and cost  
Required Skills 
• Project management (planning, scheduling, budgeting)  
• Leadership and team management  
• Risk management  
• Electrical engineering fundamentals  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• PMP certification preferred
