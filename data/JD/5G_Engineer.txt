37. 5G Engineer 
Job Summary 
A 5G Engineer designs, deploys, and optimizes fifth-generation mobile networks to deliver 
ultra-fast data speeds, low latency, and massive device connectivity. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and plan 5G network architecture (RAN, Core, Transport)  
• Deploy and configure 5G base stations (gNodeB)  
• Optimize network performance (latency, throughput, coverage)  
• Perform network slicing and QoS management  
• Conduct field testing and drive tests  
• Troubleshoot network issues and ensure service reliability  
• Ensure compliance with standards from 3GPP  
Required Skills 
• 5G NR architecture and protocols  
• RF optimization and network planning  
• LTE/4G fundamentals (as a base)  
• Tools: TEMS, Atoll, NetAct  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electronics / Telecommunications
