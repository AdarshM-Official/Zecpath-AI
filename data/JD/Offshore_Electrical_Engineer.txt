55. Offshore Electrical Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Offshore Electrical Engineer manages electrical systems on offshore platforms such as oil 
rigs and drilling units, ensuring continuous and safe operations in remote marine 
environments. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Maintain and troubleshoot offshore electrical systems  
• Manage generators, motors, and power distribution systems  
• Ensure system reliability in harsh marine conditions  
• Support drilling and production operations  
• Perform preventive and corrective maintenance  
• Ensure compliance with offshore safety standards  
• Coordinate with onshore teams and contractors  
Required Skills 
• Offshore power systems  
• Marine electrical systems  
• Fault diagnosis and troubleshooting  
• Safety and emergency response  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• Offshore safety certifications (preferred)
