66. Defense / Space Scientist (Electrical) 
Job Summary 
A Defense or Space Scientist works on highly advanced electrical systems used in defense 
technologies, satellites, communication systems, and aerospace applications. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and develop advanced electrical and electronic systems  
• Work on radar, communication, and control systems  
• Develop satellite and spacecraft electrical systems  
• Conduct testing under extreme conditions  
• Work on secure communication and defense technologies  
• Collaborate with multidisciplinary engineering teams  
• Contribute to national security and space missions  
Required Skills 
• Advanced electronics and power systems  
• Signal processing and communication systems  
• Embedded systems and control engineering  
• Research and system design skills  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech + M.Tech (Electrical/ECE/Instrumentation)  
• Selection through competitive exams and interviews
