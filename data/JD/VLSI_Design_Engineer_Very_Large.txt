29. VLSI Design Engineer (Very Large 
Scale Integration) 
Job Summary 
A VLSI Design Engineer designs and develops integrated circuits (ICs) by integrating 
millions of transistors onto a single chip, used in processors, memory devices, and SoCs. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design digital/analog IC circuits at transistor and gate level  
• Perform RTL design using Verilog/VHDL  
• Conduct simulation, synthesis, and timing analysis  
• Work on physical design (placement & routing)  
• Perform verification and validation of chip design  
• Collaborate with fabrication and testing teams  
Required Skills 
• Verilog / VHDL (HDL programming)  
• Digital electronics and CMOS design  
• EDA tools (Cadence, Synopsys, Mentor Graphics)  
• Static Timing Analysis (STA)  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech (Electronics) + M.Tech in VLSI preferred
