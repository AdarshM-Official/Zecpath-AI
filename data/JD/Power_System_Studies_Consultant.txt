83. Power System Studies Consultant 
Job Summary 
A Power System Studies Consultant performs advanced analysis of electrical power systems 
to ensure stability, reliability, and efficiency of electrical networks. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Perform load flow, short circuit, and stability studies  
• Conduct protection coordination analysis  
• Analyze system faults and grid performance  
• Optimize transmission and distribution networks  
• Provide simulation reports for EPC and utility projects  
• Use software tools like ETAP, PSS/E, PSCAD  
Required Skills 
• Power system analysis  
• Simulation tools (ETAP, MATLAB, PSCAD)  
• Electrical network modeling  
• Strong analytical skills  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• M.Tech (Power Systems) preferred
