Instrumentation & Control Engineer

Job Summary
An Instrumentation & Control Engineer manages measurement and control systems to ensure accurate monitoring and efficient operation of industrial processes.

Key Responsibilities
- Design and maintain instrumentation systems (sensors, transmitters)
- Calibrate and troubleshoot instruments
- Develop control strategies and loops (PID control)
- Integrate instruments with PLC/DCS systems
- Ensure process accuracy and stability
- Maintain compliance with standards like ISA

Required Skills
- Process instrumentation
- Control systems (PID tuning)
- Calibration techniques
- Field instrumentation knowledge

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Instrumentation / Electrical / Electronics
