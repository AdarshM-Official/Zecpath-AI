Substation Engineer

Job Summary
Responsible for the design, installation, operation, and maintenance of electrical substations.

Key Responsibilities
- Design substation layouts and equipment placement
- Install and commission transformers, breakers, and relays
- Monitor substation performance
- Ensure safety and compliance standards
- Conduct preventive and corrective maintenance

Required Skills
- Substation automation systems
- Protection schemes
- SCADA systems

Qualifications
- B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
