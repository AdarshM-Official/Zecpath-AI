62. Digital Twin Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Digital Twin Engineer creates virtual models of physical electrical systems to simulate, 
monitor, and optimize real-world operations in real time. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop digital replicas of physical systems (power plants, grids, machines)  
• Integrate real-time sensor data into virtual models  
• Simulate system behavior under different conditions  
• Predict failures and optimize performance  
• Work with IoT and cloud-based platforms  
• Collaborate with engineering and data science teams  
Required Skills 
• Digital twin platforms and simulation tools  
• IoT and sensor integration  
• Data analytics and modeling  
• Programming (Python, MATLAB)  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Mechanical / Computer Science
