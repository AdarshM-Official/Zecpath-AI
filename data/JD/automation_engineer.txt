Automation Engineer

Job Summary
An Automation Engineer designs and implements automated systems to improve efficiency, productivity, and safety in industrial operations.

Key Responsibilities
- Design automation systems using PLC, SCADA, and robotics
- Improve manufacturing processes through automation
- Integrate sensors, actuators, and control systems
- Conduct system testing, validation, and commissioning
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams
- Optimize system performance and reduce downtime

Required Skills
- PLC, SCADA, robotics
- Industrial automation design
- Problem-solving and system integration
- Programming skills (Python, C, etc. – optional advantage)

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Mechatronics / Automation
