Power Plant Engineer

Job Summary
Responsible for the operation, performance optimization, and reliability of power generation systems.

Key Responsibilities
- Monitor and control power generation systems and equipment
- Ensure optimal plant efficiency and output
- Analyze performance data and implement improvements
- Coordinate with operations, maintenance, and safety teams
- Troubleshoot electrical faults and system failures
- Ensure compliance with regulatory standards

Required Skills
- Power plant operations knowledge
- Electrical systems troubleshooting
- SCADA/DCS systems
- Data analysis

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
