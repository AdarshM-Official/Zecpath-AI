75. Engineering Manager (Electrical) 
Job Summary 
An Engineering Manager leads electrical engineering teams, ensuring technical excellence, 
innovation, and successful delivery of engineering solutions. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Manage engineering teams and workflows  
• Oversee design, development, and execution activities  
• Ensure technical quality and compliance  
• Review engineering designs and approve solutions  
• Drive innovation and process improvement  
• Coordinate across departments (mechanical, civil, automation)  
• Mentor junior engineers and team members  
Required Skills 
• Leadership and team management  
• Strong electrical engineering expertise  
• Decision-making and problem-solving  
• Cross-functional coordination  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• M.Tech preferred for senior roles
