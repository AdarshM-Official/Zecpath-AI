43. Power Electronics Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Power Electronics Engineer designs and develops electronic circuits that control and 
convert electrical power in EVs and industrial systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design converters (DC-DC, AC-DC, DC-AC inverters)  
• Develop motor drive systems and controllers  
• Work on switching devices (IGBT, MOSFET)  
• Optimize efficiency, thermal performance, and reliability  
• Perform simulation and testing of power circuits  
• Ensure compliance with electrical standards such as IEC  
Required Skills 
• Power electronics design  
• Circuit simulation (MATLAB, PLECS, LTspice)  
• Control systems and motor drives  
• Thermal management  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Power Electronics
