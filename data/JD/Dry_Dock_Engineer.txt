Role Overview: Manages ship repair, inspection, and overhaul activities during dry docking, when vessels are taken out of water.
Responsibilities:
• Plan dry dock schedules and scope of work
• Supervise hull inspection, cleaning, and coating
• Coordinate repairs of propellers, rudders, and underwater systems
• Liaise with shipyard teams and contractors
• Ensure timely completion within budget
• Maintain safety and compliance standards
Skills:
• Project coordination
• Knowledge of hull structures and coatings
• Time and cost management
Qualifications:
• Marine / Naval Architecture / Mechanical Engineering
Salary:
• $3,000 – $8,000/month
