Plant Electrical Engineer

Job Summary
A Plant Electrical Engineer is responsible for managing the overall electrical infrastructure of an industrial plant, ensuring smooth operations, safety, and efficiency.

Key Responsibilities
- Oversee plant-wide electrical systems and utilities
- Manage power distribution, transformers, and substations
- Coordinate maintenance and shutdown activities
- Monitor energy usage and implement cost-saving measures
- Lead electrical teams and contractors
- Ensure compliance with safety and regulatory standards

Required Skills
- Power distribution systems
- Team management and leadership
- Energy efficiency optimization
- Safety and compliance management

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
- Experience in plant operations preferred
