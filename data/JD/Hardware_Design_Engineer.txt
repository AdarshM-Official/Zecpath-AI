27. Hardware Design Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Hardware Design Engineer is responsible for designing and developing physical electronic 
hardware systems, including circuit boards and components. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design hardware architecture and system schematics  
• Select electronic components and ICs  
• Perform circuit simulation and validation  
• Collaborate with PCB designers for layout  
• Conduct prototype testing and debugging  
• Ensure reliability and manufacturability of hardware  
Required Skills 
• Circuit design and analysis  
• Component selection and datasheet understanding  
• Signal integrity basics  
• Simulation tools (LTspice, Proteus)  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electronics / Electrical Engineering
