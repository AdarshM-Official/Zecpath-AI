50. HVAC Electrical Engineer 
Job Summary 
An HVAC Electrical Engineer designs, installs, and maintains the electrical and control 
systems that power Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC) systems in buildings. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design electrical systems for HVAC equipment (chillers, AHUs, pumps)  
• Develop control wiring diagrams and panel layouts  
• Integrate HVAC systems with automation (BMS/SCADA)  
• Perform load calculations and power distribution planning  
• Troubleshoot HVAC electrical and control issues  
• Ensure compliance with standards like ASHRAE and IEC  
• Support installation, testing, and commissioning  
Required Skills 
• HVAC systems understanding  
• Electrical control systems  
• BMS integration  
• AutoCAD / electrical design tools  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Mechanical Engineering
