67. Electrical Testing Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Electrical Testing Engineer is responsible for testing electrical equipment and systems to 
ensure proper functionality, safety, and compliance before commissioning or deployment. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Perform functional and performance testing of electrical systems  
• Test motors, transformers, switchgear, and control panels  
• Conduct insulation, continuity, and earth resistance tests  
• Identify faults and provide corrective recommendations  
• Prepare test reports and documentation  
• Support commissioning activities on-site  
• Ensure compliance with standards like IEC and IEEE  
Required Skills 
• Electrical testing procedures  
• Use of testing instruments (multimeter, megger, relay tester)  
• Troubleshooting and diagnostics  
• Understanding of power systems  
Qualifications 
• Diploma / B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
