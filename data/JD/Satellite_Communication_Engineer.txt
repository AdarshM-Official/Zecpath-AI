39. Satellite Communication Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Satellite Communication Engineer develops and manages communication systems that use 
satellites for data transmission, broadcasting, and navigation. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design satellite communication links and systems  
• Perform link budget analysis and signal calculations  
• Configure ground stations and antennas  
• Monitor satellite network performance  
• Troubleshoot communication issues  
• Ensure compliance with international standards and regulations  
Required Skills 
• RF and microwave engineering  
• Antenna systems  
• Signal processing  
• Satellite link design  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electronics / Communication Engineering
