51. Fire Alarm System Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Fire Alarm System Engineer designs, installs, and maintains fire detection and alarm 
systems to ensure building safety and compliance with fire safety regulations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design fire alarm and detection systems (smoke, heat detectors)  
• Prepare system layouts and wiring diagrams  
• Install and commission fire alarm panels and devices  
• Perform testing, inspection, and maintenance  
• Ensure compliance with fire safety standards like NFPA (e.g., NFPA 72)  
• Coordinate with safety authorities and project teams  
Required Skills 
• Fire alarm system design  
• Electrical wiring and installation  
• Testing and troubleshooting  
• Knowledge of safety regulations  
Qualifications 
• Diploma / B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Electronics
