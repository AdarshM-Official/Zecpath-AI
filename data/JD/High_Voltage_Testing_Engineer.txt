69. High Voltage Testing Engineer 
Job Summary 
A High Voltage Testing Engineer specializes in testing and validating high-voltage electrical 
equipment to ensure safe operation under extreme electrical stress conditions. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Conduct high-voltage (HV) tests on equipment like transformers and cables  
• Perform dielectric, insulation, and breakdown voltage tests  
• Operate HV testing equipment and safety systems  
• Analyze test results and identify failure risks  
• Ensure strict adherence to safety protocols  
• Prepare detailed technical test reports  
• Support R&D and certification processes  
Required Skills 
• High-voltage engineering concepts  
• Electrical safety procedures  
• Testing equipment operation  
• Fault analysis and interpretation  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
