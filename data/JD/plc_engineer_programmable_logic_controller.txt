PLC Engineer (Programmable Logic Controller)

Job Summary
A PLC Engineer designs, programs, and maintains automation systems using PLCs to control industrial machinery and processes.

Key Responsibilities
- Develop and program PLC logic (Ladder, Function Block, Structured Text)
- Configure I/O modules, sensors, and actuators
- Troubleshoot PLC-controlled systems and resolve faults
- Integrate PLCs with SCADA/HMI systems
- Perform testing, commissioning, and validation
- Maintain documentation and backups of PLC programs

Required Skills
- PLC platforms (Siemens, Allen-Bradley, Mitsubishi)
- Electrical control systems
- Troubleshooting and debugging
- Industrial communication protocols (Modbus, Profibus, Ethernet/IP)

Qualifications
- Diploma/B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Electronics / Instrumentation
