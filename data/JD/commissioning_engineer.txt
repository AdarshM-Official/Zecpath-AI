Commissioning Engineer

Job Summary
A Commissioning Engineer is responsible for testing, validating, and bringing electrical systems and equipment into operational condition in new or upgraded power plants.

Key Responsibilities
- Perform pre-commissioning and commissioning activities
- Test transformers, switchgear, protection systems, and control systems
- Verify installation against design specifications
- Troubleshoot issues during startup
- Prepare commissioning reports and documentation
- Coordinate with EPC contractors and project teams

Required Skills
- Testing and validation
- Electrical system understanding
- Problem-solving and troubleshooting
- Knowledge of standards like IEC

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
