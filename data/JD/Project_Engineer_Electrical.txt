73. Project Engineer (Electrical) 
Job Summary 
A Project Engineer manages the technical execution of electrical engineering projects, 
ensuring design, installation, testing, and commissioning are completed on time and within 
budget. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Coordinate electrical project execution from design to commissioning  
• Review drawings, BOQs, and technical specifications  
• Monitor project progress, timelines, and resources  
• Coordinate with contractors, vendors, and site teams  
• Resolve technical issues during execution  
• Ensure compliance with standards like IEC and project specifications  
• Prepare project reports and documentation  
Required Skills 
• Electrical systems knowledge  
• Project coordination and planning  
• AutoCAD / technical drawing understanding  
• Communication and problem-solving  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
