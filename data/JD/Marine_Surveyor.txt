Role Overview: A Marine Surveyor conducts inspections of ships, cargo, and marine equipment to verify safety and compliance.
Key Responsibilities:
• Inspect ship structures, machinery, and onboard systems
• Conduct cargo inspections (damage, loading compliance)
• Perform pre-purchase and condition surveys
• Prepare detailed inspection and certification reports
• Ensure compliance with international maritime regulations
Required Skills:
• Strong inspection and reporting skills
• Knowledge of ship systems and maritime law
• Attention to detail
Qualifications:
• Marine Engineering / Nautical Science
• Surveyor certification (preferred)
Salary Range:
• $3,000 – $8,000/month
