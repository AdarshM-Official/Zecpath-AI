15. Smart Grid Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Smart Grid Engineer develops advanced electrical grid systems using digital 
communication, automation, and data analytics to improve efficiency and reliability. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement smart grid technologies  
• Integrate renewable energy sources into the grid  
• Work with IoT devices, sensors, and smart meters  
• Analyze grid data for optimization and fault detection  
• Develop demand response and energy management systems  
• Ensure cybersecurity and grid stability  
Required Skills 
• Power systems + IT integration  
• IoT and communication protocols  
• Data analytics and AI basics  
• SCADA and automation systems  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• Knowledge of smart systems or data analytics preferred
