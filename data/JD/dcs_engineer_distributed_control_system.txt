DCS Engineer (Distributed Control System)

Job Summary
A DCS Engineer is responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining distributed control systems used in large-scale industrial processes.

Key Responsibilities
- Configure and program DCS systems
- Develop control strategies for continuous processes
- Monitor plant operations through DCS interfaces
- Perform system upgrades and troubleshooting
- Work with field instruments and controllers
- Ensure process safety and reliability

Required Skills
- DCS platforms (Honeywell, ABB, Yokogawa, Emerson)
- Process control engineering
- System integration
- Troubleshooting

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Instrumentation Engineering
