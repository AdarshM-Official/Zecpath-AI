80. Energy Auditor 
Job Summary 
An Energy Auditor analyzes energy consumption in buildings and industries and 
recommends solutions to improve efficiency and reduce costs. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Conduct energy audits in buildings and factories  
• Measure and analyze electrical energy consumption  
• Identify energy losses and inefficiencies  
• Recommend energy-saving solutions and upgrades  
• Prepare audit reports and compliance documentation  
• Support ISO 50001 energy management systems  
Required Skills 
• Energy management and auditing techniques  
• Data analysis and measurement tools  
• Electrical system knowledge  
• Report writing and documentation  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• Certified Energy Auditor (CEA) preferred
