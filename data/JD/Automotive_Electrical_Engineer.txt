40. Automotive Electrical Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Automotive Electrical Engineer designs, develops, and maintains electrical systems in 
vehicles, including wiring, lighting, control systems, and electronic modules. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design vehicle electrical architecture and wiring harness systems  
• Develop and integrate electronic control units (ECUs)  
• Work on lighting, infotainment, and power distribution systems  
• Perform diagnostics and troubleshooting of vehicle electrical issues  
• Ensure compliance with automotive standards like ISO (e.g., ISO 26262 for 
functional safety)  
• Collaborate with mechanical and software teams  
Required Skills 
• Automotive electrical systems  
• CAN/LIN communication protocols  
• Wiring harness design tools  
• Diagnostics tools (OBD systems)  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Electronics / Automotive Engineering
