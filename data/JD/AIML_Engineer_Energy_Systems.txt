60. AI/ML Engineer (Energy Systems) 
Job Summary 
An AI/ML Engineer in Energy Systems applies machine learning and artificial intelligence to 
optimize power generation, consumption, and grid management. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop predictive models for energy demand and supply  
• Implement AI-based fault detection in power systems  
• Optimize energy consumption using ML algorithms  
• Work on renewable energy forecasting (solar/wind)  
• Analyze smart grid data for decision-making  
• Deploy AI models into real-time energy systems  
Required Skills 
• Machine learning (regression, classification, deep learning)  
• Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch  
• Data analytics and time-series forecasting  
• Energy systems knowledge  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Computer Science  
• AI/ML specialization preferred
