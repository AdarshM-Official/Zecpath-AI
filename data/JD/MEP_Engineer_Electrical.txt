46. MEP Engineer (Electrical) 
Job Summary 
An MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) Engineer – Electrical focuses on designing, 
coordinating, and executing electrical systems in buildings such as commercial complexes, 
hospitals, malls, and infrastructure projects. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and review electrical systems (power distribution, lighting, ELV systems)  
• Prepare load calculations, cable sizing, and panel schedules  
• Coordinate with mechanical and plumbing teams  
• Ensure compliance with standards like IEC and IEEE  
• Supervise installation and ensure quality control  
• Review drawings and BOQs (Bill of Quantities)  
• Support project planning and execution  
Required Skills 
• Electrical design for buildings  
• AutoCAD / Revit MEP  
• Load calculations and system planning  
• Project coordination  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
