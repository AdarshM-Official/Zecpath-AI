Electronics Engineer

Job Summary
An Electronics Engineer designs, develops, tests, and maintains electronic circuits and systems used in devices ranging from consumer electronics to industrial equipment.

Key Responsibilities
- Design and analyze analog and digital circuits
- Develop schematics and circuit layouts
- Test and validate electronic components and systems
- Troubleshoot circuit issues and improve performance
- Work with microcontrollers and embedded components
- Ensure compliance with standards like IEC

Required Skills
- Analog & digital electronics
- Circuit simulation (Multisim, LTspice)
- Oscilloscope, multimeter usage
- Soldering and prototyping

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electronics / Electrical Engineering
