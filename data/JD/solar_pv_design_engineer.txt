Solar PV Design Engineer

Job Summary
A Solar PV Design Engineer is responsible for designing efficient and cost-effective solar power systems for residential, commercial, and utility-scale projects.

Key Responsibilities
- Design solar PV systems (rooftop and ground-mounted)
- Perform site assessment and feasibility analysis
- Prepare system layouts, single-line diagrams (SLD), and electrical schematics
- Calculate system capacity, inverter sizing, and cable sizing
- Optimize panel placement for maximum energy generation
- Ensure compliance with standards like IEC
- Coordinate with installation and project teams

Required Skills
- PV system design tools (PVsyst, Helioscope)
- AutoCAD and electrical design
- Knowledge of solar components (panels, inverters, batteries)
- Energy yield analysis

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Renewable Energy
- Solar certifications (preferred)
