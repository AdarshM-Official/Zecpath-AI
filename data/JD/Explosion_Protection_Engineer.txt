56. Explosion Protection Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Explosion Protection Engineer designs and ensures electrical and instrumentation systems 
are safe to operate in explosive atmospheres, preventing ignition risks in hazardous industrial 
environments. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Perform hazardous area classification (Zone 0, 1, 2)  
• Design explosion protection systems (Ex d, Ex e, Ex i, etc.)  
• Select certified explosion-proof equipment  
• Conduct risk assessments and safety audits  
• Review engineering drawings and ensure compliance  
• Prepare documentation for certification and inspections  
• Investigate incidents and implement corrective actions  
• Ensure compliance with standards like ATEX and IECEx  
Required Skills 
• Explosion protection techniques  
• Hazardous area classification  
• Safety engineering and compliance  
• Risk assessment methodologies (HAZOP, HAZID)  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Instrumentation Engineering  
• Certification in hazardous area safety (preferred)
