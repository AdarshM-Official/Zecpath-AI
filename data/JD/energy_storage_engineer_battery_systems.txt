Energy Storage Engineer (Battery Systems)

Job Summary
An Energy Storage Engineer focuses on designing and managing battery systems used for storing energy in renewable and grid applications.

Key Responsibilities
- Design battery energy storage systems (BESS)
- Select appropriate battery technologies (Li-ion, etc.)
- Develop Battery Management Systems (BMS)
- Perform charge/discharge cycle analysis
- Ensure safety, thermal management, and system efficiency
- Integrate storage systems with solar/wind and grid

Required Skills
- Battery technologies and BMS design
- Power electronics
- Thermal management
- Simulation tools (MATLAB/Simulink)

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Electronics Engineering
