Shift Engineer (Power Plant)

Job Summary
Supervises plant operations during assigned shifts, ensuring safe and continuous power generation.

Key Responsibilities
- Monitor plant parameters
- Supervise operators and technicians
- Handle emergencies
- Maintain logs and reports
- Coordinate with maintenance teams
- Ensure safety protocols

Required Skills
- Real-time decision making
- Leadership skills
- SCADA/DCS operation
- Problem-solving

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
