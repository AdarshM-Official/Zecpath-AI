30. FPGA Engineer (Field Programmable 
Gate Array) 
Job Summary 
An FPGA Engineer designs and programs configurable hardware systems using FPGA 
devices for high-speed and real-time applications. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop FPGA-based designs using HDL (Verilog/VHDL)  
• Implement digital logic on FPGA boards  
• Perform simulation and debugging of FPGA designs  
• Optimize performance, latency, and power usage  
• Interface FPGA with processors, memory, and peripherals  
• Test and validate hardware functionality  
Required Skills 
• Verilog / VHDL  
• Digital design and logic circuits  
• FPGA tools (Xilinx Vivado, Intel Quartus)  
• Debugging using logic analyzers  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electronics / Electrical Engineering
