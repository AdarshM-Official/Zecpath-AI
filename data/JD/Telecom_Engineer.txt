33. Telecom Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Telecom Engineer designs, installs, maintains, and optimizes communication systems 
including voice, data, and broadband networks. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and deploy telecom networks (wired & wireless)  
• Install and configure telecom equipment (routers, switches, base stations)  
• Monitor network performance and troubleshoot issues  
• Ensure network uptime, reliability, and quality of service (QoS)  
• Perform network upgrades and expansions  
• Coordinate with vendors and field teams  
Required Skills 
• Networking fundamentals (IP, routing, switching)  
• Telecom technologies (2G/3G/4G/5G, VoIP)  
• Troubleshooting and diagnostics  
• Basic RF knowledge  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electronics / Telecommunications
