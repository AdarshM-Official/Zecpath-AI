59. Electrical Simulation Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Electrical Simulation Engineer models and simulates electrical systems to predict 
performance, optimize design, and reduce real-world testing costs. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Build simulation models for electrical systems (power, circuits, machines)  
• Perform system-level analysis (load flow, transient analysis)  
• Simulate motor drives, converters, and power systems  
• Validate designs before physical implementation  
• Use simulation data to improve efficiency and reliability  
• Collaborate with design and R&D teams  
Required Skills 
• MATLAB/Simulink  
• PSCAD, ETAP, or similar tools  
• Electrical machine and power system knowledge  
• Numerical modeling and analysis  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• M.Tech preferred for R&D roles
