Protection & Relay Engineer

Job Summary
Designs and maintains protection systems that safeguard electrical networks from faults and failures.

Key Responsibilities
- Design protection schemes for power systems
- Configure and test relays and circuit breakers
- Perform fault analysis and coordination studies
- Ensure system safety and reliability
- Conduct relay testing and maintenance

Required Skills
- Relay coordination
- Fault analysis
- Knowledge of protection devices

Qualifications
- B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
