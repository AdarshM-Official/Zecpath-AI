45. Charging Infrastructure Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Charging Infrastructure Engineer designs, installs, and manages EV charging systems and 
networks, ensuring efficient, safe, and scalable electric vehicle charging solutions. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design EV charging stations (AC & DC fast chargers)  
• Plan charging infrastructure layout and grid connectivity  
• Work with power distribution systems and load management  
• Ensure compliance with standards like IEC  
• Integrate smart charging and payment systems  
• Monitor charging station performance and reliability  
• Support installation, commissioning, and maintenance  
Required Skills 
• Power systems and electrical design  
• EV charging standards (CCS, CHAdeMO, Type 2)  
• Load management and grid integration  
• IoT and smart systems  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
