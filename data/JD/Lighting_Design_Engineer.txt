49. Lighting Design Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Lighting Design Engineer specializes in designing lighting systems that are functional, 
energy-efficient, and aesthetically pleasing for buildings and outdoor spaces. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design indoor and outdoor lighting systems  
• Perform lighting calculations and simulations  
• Select appropriate lighting fixtures and technologies (LED, smart lighting)  
• Ensure compliance with energy efficiency standards  
• Collaborate with architects and interior designers  
• Optimize lighting for visual comfort and aesthetics  
Required Skills 
• Lighting design software (Dialux, Relux)  
• Knowledge of illumination engineering  
• Creativity and design sense  
• Energy efficiency concepts  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering or related field
