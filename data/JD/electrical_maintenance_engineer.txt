Electrical Maintenance Engineer

Job Summary
An Electrical Maintenance Engineer ensures the reliability, safety, and continuous operation of electrical systems and equipment in industrial facilities through preventive and corrective maintenance.

Key Responsibilities
- Perform preventive and breakdown maintenance of electrical equipment
- Maintain motors, transformers, switchgear, panels, and drives
- Diagnose faults and implement quick repairs to minimize downtime
- Develop and follow maintenance schedules and SOPs
- Monitor equipment health and performance trends
- Ensure compliance with safety standards and procedures
- Maintain maintenance logs and documentation

Required Skills
- Troubleshooting and fault diagnosis
- Knowledge of motors, drives (VFDs), and control panels
- Maintenance planning and execution
- Basic PLC and automation understanding

Qualifications
- Diploma / B.E / B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
