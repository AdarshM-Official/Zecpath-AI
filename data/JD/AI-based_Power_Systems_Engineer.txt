89. AI-based Power Systems Engineer 
Job Summary 
An AI-based Power Systems Engineer applies artificial intelligence and machine learning to 
optimize electrical power systems, improve efficiency, and predict failures. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop AI models for power system optimization  
• Predict faults and failures in electrical networks  
• Optimize load flow and energy distribution  
• Implement AI-based predictive maintenance  
• Analyze smart grid and energy data  
• Improve renewable energy forecasting  
• Deploy AI solutions into real-time systems  
Required Skills 
• Machine learning & deep learning  
• Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch  
• Power systems engineering  
• Data analytics and forecasting  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Computer Science  
• AI/ML specialization preferred
