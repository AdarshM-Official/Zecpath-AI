88. Robotics Engineer (Electrical Focus) 
Job Summary 
A Robotics Engineer designs and develops intelligent robotic systems that combine electrical, 
mechanical, and software components for automation and AI-based applications. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design electrical systems for robots  
• Integrate sensors, actuators, and control systems  
• Develop embedded control systems for robotics  
• Program robotic movement and automation tasks  
• Test and optimize robot performance  
• Work on industrial, service, or autonomous robots  
Required Skills 
• Embedded systems and control systems  
• Sensor integration and automation  
• Basic AI/ML understanding  
• Programming (C/C++, Python)  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Robotics / Mechatronics
