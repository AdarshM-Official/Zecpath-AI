Wind Energy Engineer

Job Summary
A Wind Energy Engineer designs, analyzes, and optimizes wind power systems, including turbine performance and wind farm layouts.

Key Responsibilities
- Conduct wind resource assessment and site analysis
- Design wind farm layouts and turbine placement
- Analyze turbine performance and efficiency
- Monitor wind energy production and optimize output
- Coordinate installation and maintenance of wind turbines
- Ensure compliance with safety and environmental standards

Required Skills
- Wind modeling software (WAsP, WindPRO)
- Aerodynamics basics
- Data analysis and forecasting
- SCADA systems

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Mechanical Engineering
