76. Operations Manager (Electrical) 
Job Summary 
An Operations Manager (Electrical) ensures smooth and efficient operation of electrical 
systems, plants, or infrastructure facilities. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Oversee daily electrical operations and maintenance  
• Ensure system uptime and reliability  
• Manage maintenance schedules and teams  
• Optimize operational efficiency and energy usage  
• Handle breakdowns and emergency response  
• Ensure safety and regulatory compliance  
• Monitor KPIs like downtime, efficiency, and cost  
Required Skills 
• Operations management  
• Electrical maintenance knowledge  
• Problem-solving under pressure  
• Team leadership  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
