82. Electrical Safety Auditor 
Job Summary 
An Electrical Safety Auditor evaluates electrical installations and systems to ensure 
compliance with safety standards and prevents electrical hazards. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Conduct safety audits of electrical systems  
• Identify hazards like overloads, short circuits, and grounding issues  
• Ensure compliance with safety codes and regulations  
• Review protective systems (relays, breakers, grounding)  
• Prepare audit reports with corrective actions  
• Train staff on electrical safety practices  
Required Skills 
• Electrical safety engineering  
• Risk assessment and hazard identification  
• Knowledge of safety standards  
• Inspection and auditing skills  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• Safety certifications (NEBOSH/OSHA preferred)
