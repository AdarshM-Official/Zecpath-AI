54. Hazardous Area Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Hazardous Area Engineer ensures that electrical systems and equipment are safe for use in 
explosive or flammable environments such as oil rigs, refineries, and chemical plants. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Classify hazardous zones (Zone 0, 1, 2)  
• Select explosion-proof and intrinsically safe equipment  
• Design systems to prevent ignition risks  
• Conduct safety audits and risk assessments  
• Ensure compliance with standards like ATEX and IECEx  
• Prepare documentation for regulatory approvals  
Required Skills 
• Hazardous area classification  
• Explosion protection techniques  
• Safety engineering and risk analysis  
• Knowledge of international safety standards  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Instrumentation Engineering  
• Certification in hazardous area (preferred)
