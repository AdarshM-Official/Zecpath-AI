78. Electrical Design Consultant 
Job Summary 
An Electrical Design Consultant provides expert-level design solutions for electrical systems 
in buildings, industries, and infrastructure projects on a freelance or contract basis. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design electrical systems (power distribution, lighting, grounding)  
• Prepare single-line diagrams (SLD), layouts, and load calculations  
• Review and optimize existing electrical designs  
• Ensure compliance with standards like IEC and local electrical codes  
• Provide technical support to EPC companies and contractors  
• Solve complex design and engineering issues  
Required Skills 
• Electrical system design expertise  
• AutoCAD / Revit / design software  
• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills  
• Knowledge of building and industrial electrical systems  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• 5+ years of industry experience preferred
