38. Optical Fiber Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Optical Fiber Engineer designs, installs, and maintains fiber optic communication systems 
used for high-speed data transmission. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design fiber optic network layouts (FTTH, backbone networks)  
• Install and splice fiber optic cables  
• Perform testing using OTDR and power meters  
• Troubleshoot signal loss and connectivity issues  
• Ensure proper cable routing and protection  
• Maintain network documentation and reports  
Required Skills 
• Fiber optics principles  
• Splicing and cable installation  
• Testing tools (OTDR, fusion splicer)  
• Network troubleshooting  
Qualifications 
• Diploma / B.E/B.Tech in Electronics / Electrical
