Equipment Engineer

Job Summary
An Equipment Engineer focuses on the performance, reliability, and optimization of specific industrial equipment, ensuring minimal downtime and maximum efficiency.

Key Responsibilities
- Monitor and improve equipment performance
- Analyze failures and implement corrective actions
- Develop maintenance and calibration procedures
- Coordinate with vendors for equipment upgrades and repairs
- Maintain technical documentation and reports
- Support installation and commissioning of new equipment

Required Skills
- Equipment diagnostics and reliability engineering
- Root cause analysis (RCA)
- Preventive maintenance strategies
- Technical documentation

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Electronics Engineering
