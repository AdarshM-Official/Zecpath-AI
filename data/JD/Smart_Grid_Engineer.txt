84. Smart Grid Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Smart Grid Engineer develops modern electrical grids that use digital communication, 
automation, and real-time monitoring to improve efficiency, reliability, and sustainability. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement smart grid architectures  
• Integrate renewable energy sources into power networks  
• Deploy smart meters, sensors, and IoT-based devices  
• Monitor grid performance in real time  
• Develop demand response and load management systems  
• Improve grid resilience and fault detection systems  
• Ensure cybersecurity of grid infrastructure  
Required Skills 
• Power systems engineering  
• IoT and communication systems  
• Data analytics and automation  
• SCADA systems and smart metering  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• Specialization in power systems or smart energy preferred
