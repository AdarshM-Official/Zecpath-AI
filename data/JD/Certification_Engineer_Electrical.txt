70. Certification Engineer (Electrical) 
Job Summary 
A Certification Engineer ensures that electrical products and systems comply with national 
and international regulatory standards before being approved for use or sale. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Evaluate electrical systems for compliance with standards  
• Prepare certification documentation and reports  
• Coordinate with regulatory bodies and inspection agencies  
• Conduct product safety and performance evaluations  
• Ensure compliance with standards such as ISO and IEC regulations  
• Support product approvals and audits  
• Maintain certification records and traceability  
Required Skills 
• Knowledge of electrical safety standards  
• Documentation and compliance processes  
• Attention to detail  
• Analytical and regulatory understanding  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Electronics Engineering
