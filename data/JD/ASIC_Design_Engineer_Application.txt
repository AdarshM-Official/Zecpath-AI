31. ASIC Design Engineer (Application
Specific Integrated Circuit) 
Job Summary 
An ASIC Design Engineer develops custom-designed chips tailored for specific applications 
such as mobile processors, networking devices, and AI hardware. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design ASIC architecture and logic circuits  
• Perform RTL design, synthesis, and verification  
• Conduct power, timing, and area optimization  
• Work on chip floorplanning and layout  
• Validate chip functionality before fabrication  
• Collaborate with verification and fabrication teams  
Required Skills 
• HDL (Verilog/VHDL)  
• ASIC design flow knowledge  
• EDA tools (Cadence, Synopsys)  
• Digital and microelectronics fundamentals  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech + M.Tech in Microelectronics/VLSI preferred
