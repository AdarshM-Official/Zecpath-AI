Role Overview: A Naval Architect is responsible for the overall design, stability, safety, and performance of ships and marine structures.
Key Responsibilities:
• Design ship hull forms and general arrangements
• Perform stability, buoyancy, and hydrostatic calculations
• Conduct resistance and propulsion analysis
• Ensure compliance with classification rules (e.g., ABS)
• Collaborate with structural, mechanical, and electrical teams
• Review design drawings and approve final layouts
• Optimize vessel performance and fuel efficiency
Required Skills:
• Strong knowledge of hydrodynamics and ship stability
• Proficiency in design software (Maxsurf, Rhino, AutoCAD)
• Analytical and mathematical skills
• Regulatory knowledge
Qualifications:
• B.Tech/B.E in Naval Architecture / Ocean Engineering
Salary Range:
• Entry: $2,500/month
• Senior: $8,000+/month
