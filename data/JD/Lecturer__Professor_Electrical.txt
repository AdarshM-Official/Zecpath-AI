64. Lecturer / Professor (Electrical 
Engineering) 
Job Summary 
A Lecturer or Professor teaches electrical engineering subjects, mentors students, and 
contributes to academic research and development. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Teach undergraduate and postgraduate engineering subjects  
• Prepare course materials, lectures, and lab experiments  
• Guide student projects and research work  
• Publish academic papers and journals  
• Participate in curriculum development  
• Conduct examinations and assessments  
• Engage in institutional research activities  
Required Skills 
• Strong subject knowledge (power systems, electronics, control systems, etc.)  
• Communication and teaching skills  
• Research and academic writing  
• Mentoring abilities  
Qualifications 
• M.Tech (minimum for Assistant Professor)  
• PhD required for Professor-level roles
