57. Reliability Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Reliability Engineer focuses on improving the performance, availability, and lifespan of 
electrical systems and equipment by minimizing failures and optimizing maintenance 
strategies. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Analyze equipment failures and identify root causes (RCA)  
• Develop reliability-centered maintenance (RCM) strategies  
• Monitor system performance and downtime metrics  
• Implement predictive maintenance techniques (condition monitoring)  
• Improve equipment reliability and reduce operational risks  
• Use data analytics for performance optimization  
• Collaborate with maintenance and operations teams  
• Ensure compliance with reliability standards such as ISO  
Required Skills 
• Reliability engineering concepts (MTBF, MTTR)  
• Data analysis and predictive maintenance  
• Root Cause Analysis (RCA, FMEA)  
• Maintenance planning tools  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Mechanical Engineering
