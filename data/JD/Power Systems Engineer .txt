1. Power Systems Engineer

Job Summary

A Power Systems Engineer is responsible for designing, analyzing, and optimizing electrical

power systems including generation, transmission, and distribution networks to ensure

reliable and efficient operation.

Key Responsibilities

• Perform load flow, short-circuit, and stability analysis

• Design and evaluate power system networks

• Conduct fault analysis and system protection studies

• Optimize grid performance and reliability

• Integrate renewable energy sources into the grid

• Use simulation tools like ETAP, PSCAD, PSS/E

Required Skills

• Power system analysis

• Electrical network modeling

• MATLAB/Simulink, ETAP

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills

Qualifications

• B.Tech/B.E in Electrical Engineering

• M.Tech (Power Systems) preferred
