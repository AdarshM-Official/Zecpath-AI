68. Quality Control Engineer (Electrical) 
Job Summary 
A Quality Control Engineer ensures that electrical products, installations, and systems meet 
defined quality standards and specifications. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Inspect electrical materials and installations  
• Verify compliance with design and engineering standards  
• Conduct quality audits and inspections  
• Identify defects and non-conformance issues  
• Maintain quality documentation and reports  
• Coordinate with suppliers and project teams  
• Implement quality improvement processes  
Required Skills 
• Quality assurance methodologies  
• Electrical system knowledge  
• Inspection and documentation skills  
• Problem-solving and analytical thinking  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
