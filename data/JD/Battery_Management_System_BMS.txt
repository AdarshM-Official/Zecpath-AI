42. Battery Management System (BMS) 
Engineer 
Job Summary 
A BMS Engineer designs and develops systems that monitor and manage battery 
performance, safety, and efficiency in electric vehicles and energy storage systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and develop BMS hardware and software  
• Monitor battery parameters (voltage, current, temperature)  
• Implement state-of-charge (SOC) and state-of-health (SOH) algorithms  
• Ensure battery safety and thermal management  
• Integrate BMS with vehicle control systems  
• Perform testing, validation, and fault analysis  
Required Skills 
• Battery technologies (Li-ion, solid-state basics)  
• Embedded systems and control algorithms  
• Data analysis and modeling  
• Safety systems and thermal management  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Electronics Engineering
