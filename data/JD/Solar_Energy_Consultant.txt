79. Solar Energy Consultant 
Job Summary 
A Solar Energy Consultant provides expert guidance on designing, installing, and optimizing 
solar power systems for residential, commercial, and industrial clients. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Conduct site feasibility studies for solar installations  
• Design solar PV systems and recommend capacity  
• Perform ROI and cost-benefit analysis  
• Optimize system efficiency and energy yield  
• Guide clients on government subsidies and policies  
• Support EPC contractors in project execution  
Required Skills 
• Solar PV system design  
• Energy modeling tools (PVsyst, Helioscope)  
• Financial analysis (ROI, payback period)  
• Knowledge of renewable energy policies  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Renewable Energy
