48. Electrical Design Engineer (Buildings) 
Job Summary 
An Electrical Design Engineer (Buildings) designs electrical systems for residential, 
commercial, and industrial buildings ensuring safety, efficiency, and compliance. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design power distribution systems and lighting layouts  
• Prepare single-line diagrams (SLD) and detailed drawings  
• Perform load calculations and voltage drop analysis  
• Select electrical equipment (transformers, panels, cables)  
• Ensure compliance with building codes and standards  
• Coordinate with architects and project teams  
Required Skills 
• Electrical design and calculations  
• AutoCAD / Revit  
• Knowledge of building codes and safety standards  
• Attention to detail  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
