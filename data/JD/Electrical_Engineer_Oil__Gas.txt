53. Electrical Engineer (Oil & Gas) 
Job Summary 
An Electrical Engineer in Oil & Gas is responsible for designing, operating, and maintaining 
electrical systems used in refineries, petrochemical plants, pipelines, and processing facilities. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and maintain power distribution systems (LV/MV/HV)  
• Select and manage electrical equipment (transformers, switchgear, motors)  
• Ensure safe operation in hazardous environments  
• Perform load calculations, fault analysis, and system studies  
• Support plant operations, shutdowns, and maintenance activities  
• Coordinate with instrumentation and mechanical teams  
• Ensure compliance with standards like IEC and IEEE  
Required Skills 
• Power systems engineering  
• Industrial electrical equipment knowledge  
• Safety and compliance awareness  
• Troubleshooting and maintenance  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
