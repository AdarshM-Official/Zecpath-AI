86. EV Infrastructure Specialist 
Job Summary 
An EV Infrastructure Specialist designs and manages electric vehicle charging networks and 
infrastructure to support large-scale EV adoption. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Plan EV charging station networks (urban & highway)  
• Design AC and DC fast charging systems  
• Coordinate grid integration and load management  
• Ensure compliance with EV charging standards  
• Implement smart charging and billing systems  
• Monitor charging station performance  
Required Skills 
• Power systems and distribution networks  
• EV charging technologies  
• Load balancing and grid integration  
• IoT-based monitoring systems  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
