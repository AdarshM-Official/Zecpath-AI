Grid Operations Engineer

Job Summary
Monitors and controls the power grid in real-time to ensure stable and continuous electricity supply.

Key Responsibilities
- Monitor grid performance using SCADA systems
- Manage load dispatch and frequency control
- Respond to faults and outages
- Coordinate with generation and distribution units
- Maintain grid stability

Required Skills
- SCADA systems
- Real-time monitoring
- Decision-making under pressure

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
