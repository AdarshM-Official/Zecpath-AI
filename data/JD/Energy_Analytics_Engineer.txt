85. Energy Analytics Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Energy Analytics Engineer uses data science and analytics to optimize energy generation, 
distribution, and consumption patterns. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Analyze large-scale energy consumption data  
• Build predictive models for demand forecasting  
• Optimize energy usage in industries and grids  
• Develop dashboards for energy monitoring  
• Identify inefficiencies and recommend improvements  
• Work with renewable and smart grid datasets  
Required Skills 
• Data analytics (Python, SQL, Power BI)  
• Machine learning basics  
• Time-series forecasting  
• Energy systems understanding  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Computer Science / Data Science
