72. Regulatory Compliance Engineer 
(Electrical) 
Job Summary 
A Regulatory Compliance Engineer ensures that electrical systems, equipment, and projects 
comply with national and international laws, codes, and technical regulations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Review electrical designs for regulatory compliance  
• Ensure adherence to safety, environmental, and technical standards  
• Work with certification and inspection agencies  
• Maintain compliance documentation and audit records  
• Monitor updates in electrical regulations and codes  
• Coordinate with design, testing, and quality teams  
• Support approvals for projects and equipment installations  
• Ensure compliance with standards like ISO and IEC guidelines  
Required Skills 
• Knowledge of electrical codes and regulations  
• Documentation and audit processes  
• Analytical and detail-oriented thinking  
• Understanding of safety and environmental standards  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• Additional certification in compliance or safety preferred
