63. Research Scientist (Electrical) 
Job Summary 
A Research Scientist in Electrical Engineering works on advanced technologies, developing 
new theories, systems, and innovations in power systems, electronics, control systems, and 
emerging technologies. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Conduct advanced research in electrical systems and technologies  
• Develop prototypes and experimental models  
• Publish research papers in journals and conferences  
• Work on funded projects (government or private)  
• Develop solutions for energy, automation, and electronics challenges  
• Collaborate with academic and industrial researchers  
• Test and validate new engineering concepts  
Required Skills 
• Strong fundamentals in electrical engineering  
• Research methodology and technical writing  
• Simulation tools (MATLAB, Simulink, etc.)  
• Analytical and problem-solving skills  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech + M.Tech (Electrical Engineering)  
• PhD preferred for senior roles
