SCADA Engineer (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition)

Job Summary
A SCADA Engineer develops and manages supervisory control systems for real-time monitoring and control of industrial processes.

Key Responsibilities
- Design and develop SCADA systems and HMI screens
- Configure alarms, trends, and data logging systems
- Integrate PLCs, RTUs, and field devices
- Monitor plant operations and ensure system reliability
- Maintain and troubleshoot SCADA software and hardware
- Ensure data security and system integrity

Required Skills
- SCADA platforms (WinCC, Wonderware, Ignition)
- HMI design
- Networking basics
- Data visualization

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Electronics / Instrumentation
