Distribution Engineer

Job Summary
Handles the design and operation of electrical distribution systems delivering power to end-users.

Key Responsibilities
- Design distribution networks (LT/HT systems)
- Load forecasting and demand analysis
- Minimize power losses and improve efficiency
- Handle outage management and restoration
- Coordinate with field teams

Required Skills
- Distribution system planning
- GIS tools
- Load analysis

Qualifications
- B.Tech/B.E in Electrical Engineering
