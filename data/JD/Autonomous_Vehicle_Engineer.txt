44. Autonomous Vehicle Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Autonomous Vehicle Engineer develops self-driving technologies that enable vehicles to 
perceive their environment, make decisions, and operate safely without human intervention. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and develop autonomous driving systems (ADAS & full autonomy)  
• Integrate sensors such as LiDAR, radar, cameras, and ultrasonic systems  
• Develop perception, localization, and path-planning algorithms  
• Work on vehicle control systems and real-time decision-making  
• Test and validate autonomous systems using simulations and real-world data  
• Ensure safety compliance with standards like ISO (e.g., ISO 26262)  
• Collaborate with software, hardware, and AI teams  
Required Skills 
• Embedded systems and robotics  
• AI/ML (computer vision, deep learning)  
• Programming (Python, C++)  
• Sensor fusion and data processing  
• Control systems  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Electronics / Robotics / Computer Science  
• M.Tech in Robotics/AI (preferred)
