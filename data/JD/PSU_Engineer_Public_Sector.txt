65. PSU Engineer (Public Sector 
Undertaking) 
Job Summary 
A PSU Engineer works in government-owned organizations managing large-scale electrical 
infrastructure such as power generation, transmission, and industrial operations. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design, operate, and maintain electrical power systems  
• Manage projects in generation, transmission, and distribution  
• Ensure compliance with government regulations and standards  
• Monitor system performance and reliability  
• Handle procurement and technical evaluation of equipment  
• Supervise field operations and engineering teams  
• Support national infrastructure development  
Required Skills 
• Power systems engineering  
• Project management  
• Electrical maintenance and design  
• Knowledge of safety and regulatory standards  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• Selection through exams like GATE (commonly required)  
Key PSUs 
• NTPC  
• Power Grid Corporation of India Limited  
• BHEL  
• ONGC
