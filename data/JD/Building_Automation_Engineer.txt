52. Building Automation Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Building Automation Engineer develops and manages intelligent building systems that 
automate lighting, HVAC, security, and energy management. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement Building Management Systems (BMS)  
• Integrate HVAC, lighting, access control, and fire systems  
• Program automation controllers (PLC/DDC)  
• Monitor building performance and optimize energy usage  
• Troubleshoot automation and control system issues  
• Ensure system cybersecurity and reliability  
Required Skills 
• BMS platforms (Siemens, Honeywell, Schneider)  
• PLC/DDC programming  
• Networking and IoT basics  
• Energy management systems  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Instrumentation Engineering
