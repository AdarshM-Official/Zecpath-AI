47. Electrical Site Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Electrical Site Engineer manages on-site electrical installation, execution, and supervision 
activities during construction projects. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Supervise installation of electrical systems at site  
• Coordinate with contractors and vendors  
• Ensure work is executed as per drawings and specifications  
• Monitor project progress and timelines  
• Conduct site inspections and quality checks  
• Ensure safety compliance and proper documentation  
• Resolve on-site technical issues  
Required Skills 
• Site execution and supervision  
• Electrical systems installation  
• Problem-solving on-site  
• Communication and coordination  
Qualifications 
• Diploma / B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
