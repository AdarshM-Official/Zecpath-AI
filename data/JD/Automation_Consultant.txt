81. Automation Consultant 
Job Summary 
An Automation Consultant provides expert solutions for industrial automation systems 
including PLC, SCADA, robotics, and control systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and optimize industrial automation systems  
• Recommend PLC/SCADA architectures  
• Improve manufacturing efficiency through automation  
• Troubleshoot complex control system issues  
• Guide digital transformation (Industry 4.0 solutions)  
• Support system integration and upgrades  
Required Skills 
• PLC, SCADA, DCS systems  
• Industrial automation design  
• Control systems and robotics basics  
• System integration knowledge  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Instrumentation / Automation
