77. Technical Director (Electrical) 
Job Summary 
A Technical Director provides strategic technical leadership, guiding engineering direction, 
innovation, and long-term technology planning in an organization. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Define technical vision and strategy for electrical projects  
• Approve major engineering designs and solutions  
• Lead innovation and R&D initiatives  
• Ensure technical excellence across all projects  
• Mentor senior engineers and managers  
• Collaborate with executive leadership  
• Evaluate new technologies and industry trends  
• Ensure compliance with global standards and best practices  
Required Skills 
• Advanced electrical engineering expertise  
• Strategic thinking and leadership  
• Innovation and R&D experience  
• Decision-making at enterprise level  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech + M.Tech/PhD preferred  
• 10–20+ years of industry experience
