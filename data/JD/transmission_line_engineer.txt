Transmission Line Engineer

Job Summary
Designs and manages high-voltage transmission lines to ensure efficient electricity transfer over long distances.

Key Responsibilities
- Route selection and tower design
- Conduct line sag and tension calculations
- Oversee construction and installation
- Ensure compliance with safety standards
- Perform line inspections and maintenance planning

Required Skills
- High-voltage engineering
- Structural basics for towers
- PLS-CADD software

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
