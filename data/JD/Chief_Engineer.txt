Role Overview: The Chief Engineer is the head of the engine department, responsible for the overall operation, maintenance, and safety of all mechanical and electrical systems onboard the vessel.
Key Responsibilities:
• Manage ship’s engine room and technical operations
• Oversee maintenance of propulsion systems, generators, and auxiliary machinery
• Ensure compliance with international maritime regulations (SOLAS, MARPOL)
• Plan maintenance schedules and dry dock activities
• Manage fuel consumption and efficiency
• Lead and supervise engineering crew
• Maintain technical documentation and reports
Required Skills:
• Advanced knowledge of marine engines and systems
• Leadership and decision-making
• Troubleshooting under pressure
• Regulatory compliance expertise
Qualifications:
• Marine Engineering Degree / Class I Certificate of Competency
• Extensive sea experience (8–15+ years)
Salary Range (Approx.):
• $8,000 – $15,000/month (varies by vessel type)
