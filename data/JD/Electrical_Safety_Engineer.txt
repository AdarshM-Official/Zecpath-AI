71. Electrical Safety Engineer 
Job Summary 
An Electrical Safety Engineer ensures that all electrical systems, installations, and operations 
are designed and maintained in a way that minimizes risk to people, equipment, and the 
environment. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Develop and implement electrical safety procedures and standards  
• Conduct risk assessments (arc flash, shock hazards, fire risks)  
• Inspect electrical installations for safety compliance  
• Ensure proper grounding, insulation, and protection systems  
• Investigate electrical accidents and incidents  
• Conduct safety training for engineers and technicians  
• Ensure compliance with standards like IEC and IEEE  
• Audit workplace electrical safety systems regularly  
Required Skills 
• Electrical safety engineering principles  
• Risk assessment and hazard analysis  
• Knowledge of protective devices (relays, breakers, fuses)  
• Strong understanding of power systems  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering  
• Safety certifications (NEBOSH, OSHA preferred)
