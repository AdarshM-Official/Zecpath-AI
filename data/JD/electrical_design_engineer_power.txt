Electrical Design Engineer (Power)

Job Summary
Designs electrical systems for power plants, substations, and industrial projects, ensuring compliance with standards and project requirements.

Key Responsibilities
- Prepare single-line diagrams (SLD), wiring diagrams
- Design cable sizing, earthing, and protection systems
- Select transformers, switchgear, and circuit breakers
- Ensure compliance with standards like IEEE and IEC
- Coordinate with civil and mechanical teams

Required Skills
- AutoCAD, EPLAN
- Electrical design calculations
- Knowledge of standards and codes

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
