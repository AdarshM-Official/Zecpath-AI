41. EV Design Engineer 
Job Summary 
An EV Design Engineer focuses on designing and developing electric vehicle systems 
including motors, batteries, powertrain, and charging systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design EV architecture and powertrain systems  
• Select and integrate motors, controllers, and battery packs  
• Develop vehicle electrical layouts and system integration  
• Optimize energy efficiency and vehicle performance  
• Work on charging systems and infrastructure compatibility  
• Conduct testing and validation of EV systems  
Required Skills 
• EV powertrain systems  
• Electrical system integration  
• Simulation tools (MATLAB/Simulink)  
• Knowledge of charging standards  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electrical / Automotive Engineering
