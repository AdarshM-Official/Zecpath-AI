Industrial Electrical Engineer

Job Summary
An Industrial Electrical Engineer designs, develops, and optimizes electrical systems used in manufacturing and industrial processes to improve productivity and efficiency.

Key Responsibilities
- Design and implement industrial electrical systems
- Optimize energy consumption and reduce operational costs
- Work on automation systems (PLC, SCADA, DCS)
- Improve production efficiency through electrical upgrades
- Collaborate with mechanical and production teams
- Ensure compliance with standards such as IEC and IEEE

Required Skills
- Industrial automation (PLC, SCADA)
- Electrical system design
- Process understanding
- Energy management

Qualifications
- B.E/B.Tech in Electrical Engineering
