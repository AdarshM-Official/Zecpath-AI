36. Wireless Communication Engineer 
Job Summary 
A Wireless Communication Engineer develops and manages wireless systems that enable 
communication over radio frequencies, including mobile networks and IoT systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design and implement wireless communication systems  
• Develop protocols and optimize wireless performance  
• Analyze signal strength, interference, and bandwidth  
• Work on technologies like Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular networks  
• Test and validate wireless systems  
• Ensure compliance with standards such as 3GPP  
Required Skills 
• Wireless communication principles  
• Signal processing basics  
• Networking and RF fundamentals  
• Protocols (LTE, 5G, Wi-Fi)  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electronics / Communication Engineering
