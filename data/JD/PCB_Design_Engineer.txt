28. PCB Design Engineer 
Job Summary 
A PCB Design Engineer creates printed circuit board layouts that connect and support 
electronic components in hardware systems. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design PCB layouts from schematics  
• Perform component placement and routing  
• Ensure signal integrity and EMI/EMC compliance  
• Generate Gerber files and manufacturing outputs  
• Collaborate with hardware and manufacturing teams  
• Optimize designs for cost and performance  
Required Skills 
• PCB design tools (Altium Designer, KiCad, Eagle)  
• PCB layout techniques (multilayer boards, routing)  
• Knowledge of EMI/EMC standards  
• Attention to detail  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech / Diploma in Electronics
