34. RF Engineer (Radio Frequency) 
Job Summary 
An RF Engineer designs and optimizes radio frequency systems used in wireless 
communication such as mobile networks, satellite communication, and broadcasting. 
Key Responsibilities 
• Design RF circuits and communication systems  
• Perform RF testing, tuning, and optimization  
• Conduct site surveys and signal strength analysis  
• Optimize antenna placement and network coverage  
• Analyze interference and improve signal quality  
• Ensure compliance with regulatory standards  
Required Skills 
• RF theory and antenna design  
• Signal propagation and analysis  
• RF tools (spectrum analyzers, network analyzers)  
• Knowledge of wireless standards  
Qualifications 
• B.E/B.Tech in Electronics / Communication Engineering
